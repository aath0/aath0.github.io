#### Bio

I hold a BSc in Neuroscience from King’s College London and an MSc in Health Sciences and Technology from ETH Zürich. There, I carried out my MSc thesis, where I used machine learning, virtual reality and wearables to understand cognitive-emotional contributions to pain perception. 

I am now a PhD researcher at the CCN lab, focusing on analysis of iEEG data to study the neural mechanisms underlying the universal experience of recognising a known melody.
